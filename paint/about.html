Who We Are

ColorCraft is a painting service website created to help people explore colours and painting ideas for their homes and workplaces.

We believe that the right colour can change the appearance of a room and make a space feel more comfortable and attractive.

Our website allows visitors to learn about painting services, explore useful painting tips and select paint colours for a sample order.
